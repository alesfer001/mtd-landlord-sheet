A free quarterly MTD sheet for landlords

Record your rent and expenses for one property. The sheet sorts them into the categories a Making Tax Digital quarterly update asks for and gives you the totals to enter in your bridging software.

Download the free sheet (.xlsx)

No email needed. Works in Excel, LibreOffice and Google Sheets.

What's in it

  • One row per receipt or payment. Date, description, category, amount. The quarter fills itself in.
  • The 12 UK property categories from HMRC's quarterly update rules (4 income, 7 expense, residential finance costs), each mapped to its SA105 box.
  • Quarterly and year-to-date totals for the 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May updates. Updates are cumulative from 6 April, so both are shown.
  • Checks that flag rows with no date, no category, or a date outside the tax year.
  • A note on each category saying what usually goes in it.

A spreadsheet can't submit to HMRC on its own. You copy the totals into bridging software, which sends the update.
